share. Window focus does not change sustained CPU entitlement. Per-CPU
job-group EEVDF is now integrated; preserve the approximate fairness contract.

Preserve EEVDF eligibility, virtual deadlines and bounded default slices.

across short sleeps and migration. No automatic burst-percentile estimator or
foreground priority ladder is required. Waking does not unconditionally grant
the next slice, and the algorithm's name is not a response-time guarantee.
